Familiarize yourself with the [basic histogram](#basic-histogram) first.

The HDR histogram adds the *Gain map* section. It's a histogram that shows the range of how much gain, in stops, is added across the image. Moving the mouse over the image will show where the particular pixel falls in the gain map. Read more about [Gain maps](#gain-maps).

The *Display* section also changes to allow values above 1 to be sent to the output.

The *Output* section shows stops above white (with chevrons showing in the light scale bar under its histogram).
