For images with GPS metadata, or georeferenced files, a button is available in the Location or Georeference sections of the Info panel to open that location in a browser. Georeferenced files also have a button to open the bounds of the file.

By default, the URL leads to [geojson.io](https://geojson.io/), with the center or bounds as a GeoJSON payload. You can modify the URL to one of your choosing in the [config file](#config-file).
