Most modern photographs embed some amount of camera orientation information. Hovering over the orientation section or activating the Horizon Grid (`b`) will overlay as much information as possible about the camera orientation on the image. 

Moving the mouse over the image when the grid is active shows two curves through the current point: a curve "parallel" to the horizon and a line oriented up.

When an image has pitch/roll information, the coordinate display can be set to azimuth and elevation.

| Has Pitch/Roll | Has Bearing | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ | ✅ | Relative-to-horizon grid with compass bearings (as shown in the example) |
| ✅ | ✖️ | Relative-to-horizon grid with bearings relative to the camera's direction |
| ✖️ | ✅ | A small circle showing the compass bearing at the center |
| ✖️ | ✖️ | No horizon grid |
