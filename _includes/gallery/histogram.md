A dynamic histogram primarily for inspecting the content of the image. This page describes images in "Photo" mode; you can read elsewhere about ["Data" mode](#data-mode).

The histogram flow is read bottom to top. The bottom "Image" histogram shows raw data from the file. The currently hovered pixel is shown as vertical rules, one for each channel. Those pixel values flow into the "Display" histogram. 

While gamut is a [viewer, not an editor](#tenets), you can make some adjustments in the Display histogram to help you understand the content of the image. You can adjust black and white points on the sliders, adjust the exposure, or apply a simple S-shaped tone curve.

At the top is the "Output" histogram. This is the histogram that most applications show: the pixels after the modifications in the Display section (or [ProRAW profile](#proraw-histogram)).
