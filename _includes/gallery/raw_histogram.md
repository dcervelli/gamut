The RAW histogram augments the [basic histogram](#basic-histogram).

Starting at the bottom, the Image section now shows pixels values in their native bit depth (14-bit \[up to 16383\] in the example above) and, when the RAW is mosaiced (which most are), it shows the specific red, green, or blue value for the hovered pixel. Further, there is a Photosites section that shows the raw values of the mosaic in the vicinity of the hovered pixel. You can toggle a complete mosaic view using the eye icon in that section. Mosaic view can also be toggled using the grid button in the lower right or the `shift+M` shortcut.

An additional section, Develop, is above the Image section and includes information about the "development" of the raw. This usually involves, at least, demosaicing and applying white balance. It also displays a line indicating how many pixels of each channel were clipped during development.

Above that are the standard sections of the basic histogram.

See also: [Basic histogram](#basic-histogram), [ProRAW histogram](#proraw).
